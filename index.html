How sure is this site?

It was written from general knowledge and not checked line by line against the TempleOS documentation, so every fact carries a mark. Treat the weaker marks as leads, not citations.

  • FirmConsistent in every account the author remembers. Not checked against a source here.
  • ReportedWidely reported, or it rests on what Davis himself said; details may differ between accounts. Reported does not mean doubted: it means the detail comes from reports that have not been checked here.
  • UncertainRecalled from memory and not confirmed. Treat as unverified.
