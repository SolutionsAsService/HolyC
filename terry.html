The author

Terry A. Davis

The programmer who wrote TempleOS and HolyC, and a man living with serious mental illness.

A note on how this page is written

Davis's life was difficult and he died young. In his later years he also said many offensive and abusive things, which are well documented. This site is about the software, which is the reason anyone remembers him, so this page is short and sticks to the record. It does not repeat his offensive remarks, and it does not treat his illness as entertainment.

The work

Whatever one makes of the circumstances, TempleOS is a notable piece of work: an operating system, a compiler, an editor and a set of programs, designed around one person's ideas, largely by that one person. HolyC lives on in community compilers, and the system is still studied as an unusual, tightly integrated design.
